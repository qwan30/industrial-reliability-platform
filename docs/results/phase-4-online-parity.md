| Stream Fault | Injected Trigger | Observed Worker Action | Segment Integrity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## 4. Delivery, Retry & Offset Commit Policy

1. **Delivery Guarantee**: At-least-once delivery. Repeated executions produce identical logical UUIDv5 `window_id` and `decision_id` keys.
2. **Session-Scoped Commit**: Telemetry and status offsets are committed to Kafka only after the replay session reaches `COMPLETED` and all downstream feature vectors and score decisions are acknowledged.
3. **Retry Exhaustion**: Transient errors (timeouts, 429, 5xx) retry up to 3 attempts (delays: 0.25s, 1.0s). If exhausted, the session is marked `FAILED` with `SCORING_RETRY_EXHAUSTED` and input offsets remain uncommitted.
4. **Quarantine Routing**: Malformed payloads are hashed with SHA-256 and routed to `irp.quarantine.v1` with error diagnostics without leaking raw payload bytes.

---

## 5. Limitations & Phase 5 Hand-Off

- **Crash Recovery**: Session-scoped offset commits replay uncommitted records upon worker restart. Durable database-level deduplication and alert lifecycle management are introduced in **Phase 5: Alert Lifecycle & Persistence**.
